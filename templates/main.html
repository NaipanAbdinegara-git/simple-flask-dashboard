About this page

Welcome to my status board! Here’s a peek at what I’m currently building, what’s active, and what’s ‘put on ice’. Everything is synced automatically via Flask from my /data folder. Check the Projects tab to see my archives, or just scroll down to peek under the hood of how this site was built!

How this site stays alive

Think of this site as a little ecosystem. It's powered by Python & Flask, but instead of a heavy, clunky server, I’ve got it running on a serverless setup—essentially letting a "robot" handle the heavy lifting only when you visit.

Every time you click, a tiny bit of code wakes up, reads my local /data files, and serves you the freshest status update. No databases, no bloat, just pure code magic synced straight from my GitHub. It’s light, fast, and stays running 24/7! like a digital heartbeat for my projects!
